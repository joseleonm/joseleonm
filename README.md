### 👋 Hi there, I'm José Luis León-Medina

I'm a mathematician working at the intersection of **algebraic topology, geometry, and computation**.  
My research focuses on **applied and combinatorial topology**, with current projects on **parabolic arrangements, cohomology rings, and topological data analysis (TDA)**.

---

#### 🔍 Interests
- Topology and geometry of configuration spaces and Coxeter complexes  
- Computational topology and algebraic structures (DGAs, cohomology rings)  
- Applied and data-driven topology (TDA, persistent homology)  
- Interdisciplinary applications of mathematics in science and engineering  

---

#### 🧮 Recent Work
- Implementation of the **Parabolic DGA** for real parabolic arrangements ([`ParabolicArrangements`](https://github.com/joseleonm/ParabolicArrangements))  
- Development of computational tools for **homology and intersection products** in Coxeter complexes
